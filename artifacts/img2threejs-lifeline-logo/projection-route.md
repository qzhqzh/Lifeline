# Projection Route Decision

Projection is **not required** for this reconstruction.

The reference identity comes from explicit silhouette geometry, tube paths, node placement, solid navy facets, and emissive cyan/orange layers. It does not contain a photographic decal, painted pattern, or irregular surface image that should be baked into UVs. Reusing the front PNG as a projected texture would flatten the intended parallax and bake illustrated glow into albedo. The model will therefore use procedural geometry and separate PBR/emissive materials.
